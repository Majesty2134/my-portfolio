About Me

I'm a frontend developer based in Lagos, Nigeria, specializing in responsive, accessible, and user-focused web interfaces. I work primarily with HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript, building clean, maintainable sites without leaning on heavy frameworks or bloated templates — just code that does exactly what the project needs, nothing more.

My focus is usability and performance: interfaces that load fast, adapt cleanly across devices, and feel intuitive without needing an instruction manual. I pay close attention to the small details — spacing, motion, load times — because those are usually what separate a site that looks fine from one that actually feels good to use. Through Majcode, the studio I run, I've delivered client projects across retail, fashion, and community organizations — including larrybstyles.com — each one built around a real business problem rather than a template stretched to fit.

I handle everything from initial structure and styling to deployment, using tools like Git/GitHub, Netlify, and Vercel to keep projects version-controlled and easy to ship. Beyond client work, I mentor other developers learning JavaScript, which keeps me sharp on fundamentals and forces me to explain why something works, not just how. I'm currently open to new projects and collaborations — freelance, contract, or team-based — where I can keep building real things for real users. Outside of development, I produce music, which feeds back into my work more than people expect: both disciplines reward patience, iteration, and knowing when something is actually finished versus just done.
